# Surface brief — the two public sites (Indies Gallery, Old East Indies)

**Mode:** Experience (the gallery's works and the shop's prints lead; the homes persuade inside that grammar).
**Scope:** every public page of both sites, both languages, the shared header, footer and chat launcher. The admin
has its own brief.

**Audience and job:** collectors, institutions, designers and heritage buyers judge an original and ask the dealer on
WhatsApp or email; Instagram and Bali visitors buy a print in a few taps. Most arrive on a phone; desktop visitors
are collectors and designers at a desk.

**What hurt (the user, 2026-10-11):** both sites read as generic AI "luxury" and not expensive; desktop held in a
1340 px column. **Must stay:** Cormorant Garamond and Karla (the client's pick); the placeholder logos until the real
ones arrive (the doubled wordmark beside them goes); every behaviour, price rule, access rule and word in the lexicons.
**Feels wrong:** dark and moody, stark white minimal, vintage or colonial.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Nothing is framed. Every work and every region is held by flat colour, the colourist's wash laid along a
map's borders, never by a box, a mat or a hairline frame. It refuses the category's cream page with a serif
headline, a tracked-caps eyebrow and a framed grid.

OWN-WORLD: Copperplate ink for all type on a cool mineral paper ground; three historic pigments, verdigris, gamboge
and rose madder, each in three locked strengths (tint, wash, full) mixed only into the paper. One pigment plus ink
per view; rose marks the one action. The gallery lives in verdigris, the shop in gamboge, each in
its own pigment's strengths; the band that bridges to the sister site wears the sister's pigment, so
colour names the site. Cormorant at display
scale, Karla for everything read or clicked, sentence case, no eyebrows, no shadows, square edges. Records are ruled
running lists, facts tight right in tabular figures. Full width at every desktop size: fluid gutters, grids that
gain columns.

STORY: A collector lands on a sheet larger and clearer than any dealer site shows it, reads its record like a
catalogue entry and asks on WhatsApp. A Bali visitor sees prints hung on sunlit colour and adds one to the bag in a
few taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Gallery home at 1440: the header on the paper; the newest original, uncropped and large, set across
the edge where a verdigris wash field (the upper part) meets the paper; the headline in Cormorant near 6rem on the
field at left; caption, then the rose action beneath; the owner's facts as plain Karla lines. The shop home is the
same structure in gamboge, its lead print and Shop prints in rose.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: a work's colour washes in over its engraved line once as it enters the view
(grayscale to full colour, scroll-linked, ease-out); on hover or focus a card's window, clear at rest so the
work's paper meets the page's, takes the site's pigment (tint). Reduced motion: shown complete, no movement.

FORM: Outline Colour, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key dbb8b833.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Changes after the first render (2026-10-12)

- The gamboge and madder washes moved earthier (ochre `#d1a752`, rose-terracotta `#cf9a96`) and the shop footer's
  saffron to `#c9963a`: the first values read as mustard and baby pink, sweet rather than expensive.
- A card's window rests on a neutral deeper paper (`#e6e9e2`) and takes the pigment's tint on hover: the gamboge tint
  at rest read as a cream mat around every print.

- Finish review round 1 (2026-10-12, verdict fix): one pigment per site (the green, ochre and pink
  stack read as a template), the bridge band in the sister's colour; windows clear at rest with no
  padding, sheets on one baseline, the tint on hover only; the outline drawn — the full pigment along
  a field's lower edge; the item sheet at its true shape up to 86svh; home grids of six that never
  leave an empty slot; "Shop by" at the size of the words it opens; the wash-in complete once a work
  has wholly entered the view.
