# Surface brief — the two public sites (Indies Gallery, Old East Indies)

**Mode:** Experience (the gallery's works and the shop's prints lead; the homes persuade inside that grammar).
**Scope:** every public page of both sites, both languages, the shared header, footer and chat launcher. The admin
has its own brief.

**Audience and job:** collectors, institutions, designers and heritage buyers judge an original and ask the dealer on
WhatsApp or email; Instagram and Bali visitors buy a print in a few taps. Most arrive on a phone; desktop visitors
are collectors and designers at a desk. One man owns both: two storefronts selling different things, each to look
premium in its own way.

**What hurt (the user, 2026-10-11):** both sites read as generic AI "luxury" and not expensive; desktop held in a
1340 px column. **Then (2026-10-12, as the owner's proxy, "personal preference from the client"):** the colour to be
cleaner, more exclusive and premium, and the shop's pages tidied. **Must stay:** Cormorant Garamond and Karla (the
client's pick); the placeholder logos until the real ones arrive; full width on a desktop; every behaviour, price
rule, access rule and word in the lexicons. **Feels wrong:** dark and moody, stark white minimal, vintage or
colonial.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ivory and ink. Two shops on one clean base, colour spent only as a small accent. Space, restraint and large
calm type carry the expense: fewer, larger things. It refuses the category's beige-and-gold "luxury", the colour
band behind every heading and the framed grid.

OWN-WORLD (the client's palette, chosen from a board of four, 2026-10-12): near-black ink for all type; muted ink
for the second voice; square edges; sentence case; no eyebrows. **The gallery:** ivory paper `#f7f5f0`, a cool stone
`#eeebe4` for the few panels, a deep green `#2d5b52` on the one action a view asks for, an ink footer `#1b1c1a`. An
antique lies uncropped on the paper with no frame, no tile and no shadow; on hover its window takes the stone.
**The shop:** a warmer white `#faf8f4`, a warm-stone panel `#e9e1d4` behind the home's hero only, every print hung on
an even stone tile `#efeae1` at its own shape with one soft, low shadow, bronze `#8a5a2b` on the one action, an
espresso footer `#2a211b`. A page's head sits on the paper on both sites. The band that bridges to the sister site
wears the sister's stone, so the ground names the site. Records are ruled running lists, facts tight right in
tabular figures. Full width at every desktop size: fluid gutters, grids that gain columns up to four.

STORY: A collector lands on a sheet larger and clearer than any dealer site shows it, lying on ivory like a sheet on
a dealer's table, reads its record like a catalogue entry and asks on WhatsApp. A Bali visitor walks a wall of
prints, each hung on its own stone tile, and adds one to the bag in a few taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Gallery home at 1440: the header on the paper; the newest original, uncropped and large, beside the
headline in Cormorant near 6rem on the same ivory; the owner's facts as plain Karla lines; Explore the collection in
the green. Shop home at 1440: the headline on the warm-stone panel, the lead print hung across the panel's lower
edge with the print shadow, Shop prints in bronze, then the best sellers on even tiles.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: a print lifts a few pixels off its tile under the pointer or focus, its shadow
deepening (the shop). An antique's colour washes in over its engraved line once as it enters the view (grayscale to
colour, scroll-linked) and its window takes the stone on hover (the gallery). Reduced motion: no lift, no wash; shown
complete.

FORM: Ivory and ink, option A of the palette board (phase 18), on the phase 17 structure (Outline Colour, seed key
dbb8b833): full width, the Crossing opening, ruled records, no eyebrows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## History

- Phase 17 (2026-10-12): Outline Colour. Nothing framed; every region held by a flat pigment (verdigris, gamboge,
  rose madder) with its outline stripe; one pigment per site. The structure stayed; the colour was replaced in phase
  18 at the client's request.
- Phase 18 (2026-10-12): the client chose Ivory and ink; the colour bands and outline stripes retired; the shop's
  prints moved onto even tiles (`PrintTile`); the shop's grids, product page, home, collections and stores tidied.
