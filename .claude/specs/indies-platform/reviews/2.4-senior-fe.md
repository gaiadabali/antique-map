## The architect's open finding: the hold link. Follow-up, not a blocker

`purchase-states` gives `reserve` the href `/enquire?item=1001&topic=hold`. `hold` isn't an `EnquiryTopic`. C10's `form.topic` is a bare `string`, so the URL parses, but `FormVM.topic.selected: EnquiryTopic` can't carry it, and no C10 form kind or C2 surface posts `hold.request`. The same is true of two other writes a panel links to: `quote.proforma`, where `item-price-on-request` links to `/trade?item=1001`, a CMS `page`; and a guest's `quote.request` where `accounts.retailers` is off. Three C6 operations have a C13 address but no page that posts them. With JavaScript off, a buyer can't reach any of them, because the panel is `Streamed`.

It doesn't block v1.1, for three reasons. The defect predates it (the href is identical at e52752e). No lane consumes it. The fix only adds members: new `FORM_KINDS`, `FormVM` fields and fixture hrefs. But it must land **before 11.3/11.4 dispatch**, because the purchase-panel matrix fixtures and the loaders build against these hrefs: F1.
