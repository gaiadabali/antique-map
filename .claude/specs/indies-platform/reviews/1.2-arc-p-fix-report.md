## Decisions

- **lookupToken:** moved to an HttpOnly `order_access` cookie (C13 `ORDER_ACCESS`). The email link answers 303 to the clean order page; `href('order')` takes the number only. `order`, `pay` and `quote` are `sensitive`: the proxy answers them with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`. A pay-link or quote token is the capability its own page URL already carries.
- **CSRF:** `EngineRoute.sameOrigin`. Cookie-authenticated writes check `Origin`/`Sec-Fetch-Site`. The cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax.
- **Webhooks:** per seller for payments and shipping. Fulfilment stays keyed by `[provider]`, because its providers are brand-level.
- **Purchase panel:** state, price and actions are one union, so impossible panels don't compile. Adds a `pay` action, an `inMyBag` state, an `offerOnly` price, the `unverified` reason and `addToBag: true | { unavailable }`.
