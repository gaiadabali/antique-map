## Gaps in 6.4 as dispatched

**A. The gallery's invoice page is on our site.** [PAYMENTS.md](../../../../docs/PAYMENTS.md) §6's
table still says "Stripe Invoicing / Payment Links for inquire → pay". Those pages are
hosted by Stripe. The buyer must pay on the gallery's own `/pay/{token}` page, in the
gallery's design, with the Stripe Payment Element embedded, and with bank transfer
instructions as the other method. This page is the bridge from the chat, so it is a
designed surface with its own states, not a leftover of checkout:

- open, with the "On hold until {due date}" status
- paid
- expired or voided
- bank transfer pending

Staff build the invoice on a phone (24.5) and send its link into the buyer's WhatsApp
chat from that phone (`wa.me` share). No WhatsApp API is needed for this.

**B. 6.4.e's phase list misses four phases** that assume gallery checkout or offers:

- **25.1.b, 25.1.c** — authorise-then-capture for unique items and Checkout Sessions held
  against a checkout lock. An original no longer goes through checkout. The gallery's
  Stripe use is the invoice page alone.
- **28.1.c, 28.2.b, 28.2.d** — cart merge on the gallery, and the account's "my offers
  (counter countdown), holds, price requests". Cut or reduce these to invoices,
  viewings and consignments.
- **33.1** — the gallery's `cart`, `checkout` and `order-lookup` loaders, the
  viewer-relative purchase states, and the `hold` and `quote` form actions.
- **38.2.b and phase 38's Done when** — "an accepted offer → a working payment link"
  becomes "a WhatsApp-negotiated invoice → a working pay page".

**C. One stock pool for the shop at launch.** Docs that assume several shop locations:

- COMMERCE.md §4 (stock locations as data, the "In the showroom now" badge)
- COMMERCE.md §8 (the routing rung "showroom first for Bali")
- EXPERIENCE-SHOP.md (the "In the showroom now" badge and filter, the showroom-QR
  "You're in the showroom" view)
- TASKS.md 24.4 (transfers between locations, the showroom stock view)

What stays:

- showroom pickup of online orders (S4), served from the one pool
- recording a showroom sale against the pool (24.4.c), and low-stock alerts
- QR labels that open the product page

Recommendation, ARC to decide: keep `stock_levels` keyed by location in the schema, with
**one** shop location configured. The POS phase then adds locations without a migration,
and launch's code reads a single row. The gallery's originals are not affected: their
Singapore or Jakarta location still routes the seller.

**D. A v2 item for the POS.** Replace v2.12 ("A showroom till on the same stock (QRIS)")
with: *The Bali shops on our engine.* It covers:

- the shops as stock locations with their own logins

It overlaps v2.7's trade portal for the same partners. The two may merge when they are
