## Confirmed sound

- **Money (C5).**
  - `CURRENCY_EXPONENT` is the engine's own table, IDR 0, and lives in C1 only; C5 imports it and
    never redeclares it.
  - `Money` is readonly, so `*= 1.1` does not compile.
  - The one sanctioned double rounding (`fx-conversion` then `market-unit-price`) is named, and
    both roundings are recorded.
  - An estimate is rounded half-even once, to a whole major unit, and is never stored, summed or
    sent back.
  - `formatMoney` fixing its own fraction digits closes the ICU hydration gap.
  - `_OnlyTheBidIsAClientAmount` still holds with the three want-list operations added.
- **C7.** `MethodCapability.presentation` and `PaymentOptionView.{family, presentation}` are
  asserted equal across C1, C6 and C7 (`_FamilyIsC1s`, `_PresentationIsC7s`).
  `PaymentStarted.dailyCapWarning` is a server-read boolean from the dated limits.
- **`BuyerOrderStatus`.**
  - It is derived by one pure function from the order status, the paying attempt (else the
    latest) and the shipments.
  - A dispute cannot reach it (`_NoDisputeForTheBuyer`), and no summary carries a payment state
    (`_NoAttemptStateOnTheSummary`).
  - The poll lost its raw `payment`.
  - A late payment that was refused reads `refunded` rather than `not-paid`. That is true for the
    buyer, and the rule order gives it.
- **D39 want lists.**
  - Double opt-in is confirmed by a POST, so a mail scanner that follows the GET link confirms
    nothing.
  - An address's receipt is `{ holder, received }` alone (`_AddressReceiptAdmitsNothing`).
  - An unauthenticated repeat changes nothing and only resends the link, within
    `WANT_LIST_EMAIL_LIMIT`. This is the self-review fix, and it is right.
  - Stopping a list erases it; `WANT_LIST_PENDING_DAYS` has its purge job (DEPLOYMENT.md §5).
  - Event facts are PII-free (`_WantListDataIsPiiFree`).
  - Email goes only from the outbox.
  - The C1 rule (`retention.wantList` ⇒ `accounts.buyers` ∧ `retention.emailWantList`) is in
    `validateBrandConfigs()`'s list, BRANDS.md §4 matches, and the emporium's `supports` omit the
    account kind. C10 gates `wantList` on `retention.emailWantList` and `wantLists` on
    `retention.wantList`.
- **C12.**
  - Prices are per market (`MarketPrice[]`, disjoint destinations).
  - The listing → status/price/canBuy rule is a first-match table.
  - `sellsTo` exposes a consequence, never the export status or location.
  - The prints feed replaces the whole copy, so a withdrawn print simply drops out and an empty
    feed removes the link.
  - `IsPublicShape` covers both webhooks and both list responses.
  - `AbsoluteUrl` rejects a root-relative path.
- **C11.** Leads and kept want lists are counted from `DOMAIN_ANALYTICS_EVENTS`, never the beacon.
  `/api/x/collect` stamps `sessionId` (daily salt), `anonymousId` (consent only) and `market`, and
  the page cannot send them.
- **The D36 delta.**
  - `EnquiryTopic` has no business topic (`_NoBusinessTopic`).
  - A guest's `quote.request` answering `not-offered` where `accounts.retailers` is on is correct.
    The handler can enforce it from what it already holds: `hasModule(brand,
    'accounts.retailers')` plus a session whose customer is an approved retailer. Nothing
    client-supplied decides it.
  - Only an approved partner can sign in on such a brand (D34), and the gallery's `supports` omit
    `accounts.retailers`, so its institutions and businesses still quote as guests.
  - `not-offered` is in every operation's `Always`, so the answer admits nothing about an address.

## Blockers (fix before v1.1 is signed)

| # | Where | Finding → change | Owner |
|---|-------|------------------|-------|
| B1 | `domain/src/contracts/want-lists.ts:13-19,51-54`; `http/src/manifest/commerce.ts:146-153`; `domain-events.ts:133-146`; TASKS.md 9.4.c/e | **An address's list token cannot reach the emails the contract says carry it.** The token is "random, stored hashed", and 9.4.e checks that an email list "stores no more than its hashed token". Yet "every email about a list carries its token": the confirmation, the `wantList.repeated` resend, every alert weeks later, and each alert's RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` URL. NTF writes those emails from outbox rows that carry only `{subject, holder}`, and a hash cannot be reversed. As written, only the email sent in the subscribing transaction could hold the token, and even that one is sent after commit from the outbox. The implementing lane (29.1, NTF 20.3) cannot build this without a decision, and the choice changes SCH's DDL (9.4.c). **Choose one and write it into C6/C13 and 9.4.c/e:** (a) the token is derived rather than stored, `HMAC(secret, listId ‖ tokenVersion)`, recomputed by NTF for each email and checked by the link handler, with the version bumped only by erasure; or (b) NTF mints a new token for each email into a `want_list_tokens` table (hash, list, expires with the list). A token must never go into the outbox row: it is a credential, and `domain_events` is kept and replayed. The same question applies to `APPLICATION_ACCESS` (`auth.ts:62`) and the newsletter's tokens; settle it once for all three. | ARC (C6, C13), SCH (9.4.c), NTF |

## Should-fix

| S1 | `results.ts:30-40`; `storage.ts:116-120`; CONTENT-MODEL.md §4 | **The `idempotency_keys` DDL contradicts its own rule.** The primary key is `(scope, key)`, with scope "the operation and the caller it binds". The rule then says the same key "from another caller … answers `invalid`". With the caller inside the key, another caller's row never conflicts, so that request runs as new and `invalid` can never happen. No first answer leaks either way, but SCH will build one of two behaviours. Make the primary key `(operation, key)` with `caller_ref` and `request_sha256` stored beside it, compared on conflict. State two edge cases. A guest who signs in mid-flow changes caller (cart → customer); a retry after that is `mismatch`, and the page mints a new key. A guest with no cart cookie binds to "none" by the request hash alone. That is acceptable only because a key is a per-render UUID never exposed off the page, so say so. | ARC (C6), SCH |
| S2 | `storage.ts:116-120`; COMPLIANCE.md §7; DEPLOYMENT.md §5 | **Stored responses have no retention.** `idempotency_keys` stores whole answers, among them `CheckoutView.contact.values`, `QuoteView.buyer.taxId` (an NPWP) and token-bearing views (`offerToken`, `returnToken`). Nothing purges them, and they are outside the 28.4 export/erase flow. Add a TTL (for example 7 days, longer than any retry), a daily sweep in DEPLOYMENT.md §5, and a line in COMPLIANCE.md §7. | S3 | `paying.ts:33-38,52-57`; `payments/src/contract.ts:96-110,152-153` | **A session "cut to end before the lock or hold" can go below the provider's minimum.** Stripe Checkout's `expires_at` has a 30-minute floor, and VAs have floors too. A pay link on a short hold, or a lock near `maxLifetime` after method switching, can leave a remaining window shorter than that floor. The adapter then either breaks `SessionInput.expiresAt` ("always before the reservation's end"), so the payment window outlives the lock (PAYMENTS.md §1 rule 4, falling back to the rule-5 refund), or `createSession()` fails. Give the attempt a lease: `created_at` plus `DOMAIN_TX_TIMEOUTS.port`. An order with nothing to ship (a digital gift card, `OrderDeliveryVM` `none`) has no path out of `paid`. Rewrite the rules as a table keyed by the C8 order state, with `fulfilling`'s sub-cases in order and a fallback of `shipped`, and say where a nothing-to-ship order ends. The 19.1 `buyerOrderStatus()` test should iterate `ORDER_STATUSES` × `PAYMENT_STATUSES` × {no shipment, pickup ready, in transit, partly delivered, exception}. | ARC (C6), DOM 19.1 |
| S6 | `payments/src/contract.ts:132-136`; `payment-vocabulary.ts:93-121,191-218` | **Unit conversion is written down on `SessionInput.charge` only.** Every `Money` that crosses an adapter is C5 units: event `amount`s and `refundedTotal`, `ProviderState.paid/refundedTotal`, `RefundRequest.amount`, `capture(amount)`, `MethodCapability.cap` and `LineSummary.amount`.
