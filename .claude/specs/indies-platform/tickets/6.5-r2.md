## Decision (Opus, orchestrator-approved scope)
The proxy **drops the query string** on a surface rewrite (`engine/packages/http/src/proxy/decide.ts`, `case 'surface'`), so `/order/{number}?t=` can never deliver the token, even once registered. The routing design already answers this for tracking: **the token is the whole credential and lives in the path** (`tracking: 'track/[token]'`, and `orders_tracking_token_hash_idx` is UNIQUE). So:

- New surface **`order`**: `/order/{token}` (en `order`, id `pesanan`), and `/order/{token}/simulate` for the simulator. It is **sensitive** (the proxy then sends `no-referrer` and `noindex` itself).
- The order is looked up by `sha256(token)` against the unique hash. No number in the URL, no `?t=`.
- The simulator page needs **no attempt id from the URL**. Its action takes the order's own latest attempt in state `open`/`pending` from the database, after the token check. So a guessable `{number}-{n}` can never be settled by someone without the token. This was review finding 1.
