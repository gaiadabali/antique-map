## The defect
Works are cached with stale-while-revalidate (the `'max'` profile in `@engine/cache`). When the owner marks a work **sold** (or on hold, or available again), the **first** visit after the change still renders the stale page — "Price on request" and "Ask about this" on a sold piece; only the next visit is right. EXPERIENCE §9 wants a status change to show at once.
