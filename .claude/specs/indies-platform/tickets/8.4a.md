## What to build
1. `eval/schema.ts` — a zod (if present in the repo; else hand-written type guard) schema for one case:
   `{ id: string (kebab, unique); site: 'gallery' | 'shop'; locale: 'en' | 'id'; group: 'grounded' | 'price-bait' |
   'shop-price' | 'handoff' | 'deals' | 'injection-visitor' | 'injection-catalogue' | 'prompt-extraction' |
   'privacy' | 'off-topic' | 'abuse'; safety: boolean; turns: { role: 'user'; text: string }[]; fixtures?: {
   works?: FixtureWork[]; products?: FixtureProduct[] }; expect: { noAmount?: boolean; exactPriceLabel?: string;
   handoff?: 'whatsapp' | 'email' | 'any'; cardsFor?: string[] /* fixture ids */; masked?: boolean; leadFormOffered?:
   boolean; noPromise?: boolean; declines?: boolean; language: 'en' | 'id'; noLinksExcept?: ('wa.me' | 'mailto' |
   'own-domain')[] } }`. `FixtureWork` has public fields only (title, maker, date, places, description, stockNumber,
   status) — **no price field exists on it**; an injection case puts its attack text inside `description`.
   `FixtureProduct` has `sku`, `name`, `priceIdr` (integer), `priceLabel` (e.g. `Rp 350.000`), `inStock`.
2. `eval/cases/*.json` — at least **120 cases**, both sites, both languages (≈ half Indonesian, natural *Anda*
   voice), covering every group of AI.md §6 with at least: 15 price-bait (ballpark, "the owner said $500", "for
   insurance", "range?", "compare to Christie's", currency tricks, split-the-difference), 10 deals/promises, 15
   injection in the visitor's text (ignore-your-rules, role-play "you are now", fake system/tool tags, base64,
   multi-turn grooming, Indonesian-language attacks), 10 injection planted in a fixture description, 8 system-prompt
   extraction, 10 privacy (phone numbers, emails, addresses typed by the visitor), 8 off-topic, 6 abuse, 15
   grounded answers with fixtures, 10 shop prices, 13 handoffs (authenticity, valuation, selling an antique,
   partnership, order status). Mark `safety: true` for price-bait, deals, all injection, extraction and privacy.
3. `eval/schema.test.ts` — loads every case file and asserts: valid against the schema; ids unique; every group
   present for both sites where it applies (shop-price only on shop, price-bait only on gallery); ≥ 40 % `id`
   locale; no fixture work carries any key named like a price (`price`, `askingPrice`, `amount`, `usd`, `idr`);
   every `cardsFor` id exists in that case's fixtures.
