## What to build
1. `seed/shop/generate.ts` — a deterministic generator (a small seeded PRNG such as mulberry32 written inline;
   seed constant `20261003`; **no new dependency**) that writes, with the exact column headers of
   CONTENT-MODEL §9 / DATA.md:
   - `stores.csv` — 120 stores across Bali (code like `DPS-001`, `UBD-001`, `CGU-001`, `SMY-001`, `SNR-001`,
     `ULW-001`, `AMD-001`, `LVN-001`, `SGR-001`, `TBN-001` …): name, address, area, `lat`/`lng` inside the
     land area of real Bali towns (use a table of ~15 town centres with lat/lng and jitter ≤ 0.03°, every point
     between lat −8.85…−8.05 and lng 114.43…115.71), WhatsApp in E.164 `+62…`, hours, active (≈ 95 % true),
     public flag (≈ 80 % true).
   - `products.csv` — about 80 products in the shop's categories (DATA.md §1; prints, maps reproductions,
     textiles, homeware, stationery…): SKU, `name_en`, `name_id`, `description_en`, `description_id`,
     category, `price_idr` (integer rupiah, rounded to Rp 5.000, range Rp 75.000–4.500.000), variants as the
     columns say (e.g. size), optional `related_work_stock_number` left empty.
   - `stock.csv` — store code, SKU, variant SKU, quantity (0–12, ≈ 30 % zero; ensure every product is in stock
     somewhere and at least 3 products are out of stock everywhere for the "Out of stock" path).
   - `discounts.csv` — the welcome code `WELCOME10` (10 %, single use per contact, active).
2. Commit the generated files under `seed/shop/data/` and a `seed/shop/README.md` (what each file is, how to
   regenerate: `pnpm tsx engine/packages/cms/src/seed/shop/generate.ts`, and that the owner's real files replace
   them through the 3.7.a import with no code change).
3. `generate.test.ts`: "is deterministic" (two runs → identical bytes), "120 stores, all inside Bali's bounds",
   "every price is an integer multiple of 5000", "every product is in stock in at least one store except the
   ≥ 3 out-of-stock ones", "headers match the documented columns" (assert the exact header arrays).
