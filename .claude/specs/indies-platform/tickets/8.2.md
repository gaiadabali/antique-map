## What to build
1. **8.2.a** The panel on both sites, opened from the header entry point: streams the reply as the §2.2 events
   arrive; an "AI assistant" disclosure always visible (§3.5); 3–4 suggested starts per site and locale (lexicon);
   when opened from an item or product page it sends the item's public id as context (the server resolves it — the
   client never sends titles or prices) and shows "Asking about: <title>". The panel's JavaScript loads **only on
   open** (dynamic import from the entry point) so pages that never open the chat ship none of it.
2. **8.2.b** The handoff card from the server's `handoff_link` event (WhatsApp, email — render the server's URL;
   never build a link client-side). The lead form appears **only** after the visitor taps "Leave my details" (the
   server's consent step, §4); the details go to `/api/x/chat/consent`, never into the chat message. Clear states
   for rate-limited (with retry time), off (kill switch: a short message plus the WhatsApp/email handoff), Turnstile
   failed, too long, and network/error. Links only to our domains, `wa.me`, `mailto:` — the client re-checks.
3. Accessibility: focus moves into the panel on open and back to the trigger on close; Escape closes; the log is
   `aria-live="polite"` (the shell has it); every control has a label from the lexicon; usable at 390 px.
