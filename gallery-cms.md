# What the Old East Indies CMS left to the gallery

Written 2026-11-18 by TASKS.md 23.1 (`cms-shop` and its database), at the owner's request: a record
of everything today's one CMS holds that belongs to **Indies Gallery**, which the shop's own CMS
(`engine/packages/cms-shop`) therefore does **not** take. Today's app (`engine/apps/web`, CMS
`engine/packages/cms`) keeps serving all of it unchanged until phase 26. **Phase 25's `cms-gallery`
(TASKS.md 25.1) takes each item below** into `engine/packages/cms-gallery`; what both CMSes need
moves into `engine/packages/cms-core` instead, never copied (PLAN-V2 §3, §7).

## Collections

| What                                                                                              | Where it lives today                                                          | Note for 25.1                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `works` (the antiques: record, object, staff cataloguing, references, public id, dates and sizes) | `engine/packages/cms/src/collections/works/**`                                | with its hooks `cms/src/hooks/work-*.ts` and validators `cms/src/validators/work-*.ts`                |
| `makers`                                                                                          | `cms/src/collections/makers/**`                                               | validators `maker-*.ts`; `hooks/vocabulary-invalidate.ts`                                             |
| `places` (the gazetteer, historical names, ancestry)                                              | `cms/src/collections/places/**`                                               | validators `place-*.ts`                                                                               |
| `masters` (the private archive masters, their bucket)                                             | `cms/src/collections/masters/**`                                              |                                                                                                       |
| `terms` (subject, grade and the other gallery vocabularies)                                       | `cms/src/collections/terms/**`                                                | the shared vocabulary access is now `@engine/cms-core/access/vocabulary`                              |
| `pages`' gallery fields — intro, hero, body, curated works, `kind` (page, story, collection)      | `cms/src/collections/pages/index.ts`, `kinds.ts`                              | the shop's pages (sections, preview) are `@engine/cms-shop/collections/pages`                         |
| `media`'s master link (`masterField`, `matchItsMaster`) and the derivatives backfill CLI          | `cms/src/collections/media/master.ts`, `derivatives-cli.ts`, `public-file.ts` | the derivative pipeline itself is shared: `@engine/cms-core/collections/media/pipeline`               |
| `leads` of kind `sell` (sell to us) and `ask` about a work (`items: 'works'`)                     | `cms/src/collections/leads/index.ts`                                          | the shop's leads are `ask`, `partnership`, `contact`, `chat` about `products`                         |
| `chat-sessions` asking about works (`items: 'works'`)                                             | `cms/src/collections/chat-sessions/index.ts`                                  | `chatSessionsCollection({ items })` takes the slug                                                    |
| `products.relatedWork` (a relationship into `works`)                                              | `cms/src/collections/shop.ts` (today's binding of the shop's factories)       | the shop now keeps `originalStockNumber`, text, resolved through the gallery's public read API (23.5) |
| `partners.site`'s `gallery` option                                                                | `cms/src/collections/shop.ts`                                                 | the shop's partners carry no `site`                                                                   |

## Admin

| What                                                                                                                       | Where it lives today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Catalogue Wall (`/admin/wall`, works and products as cards)                                                            | `cms/src/admin/wall/**`; its stylesheets `engine/apps/web/src/app/(payload)/admin/wall*.css`                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| The sidebar's folded "Indies Gallery — antiques" section (wall, works, makers, places, terms, masters) and its group label | `cms/src/admin/nav/plan.ts`; the label is `ADMIN_GROUPS.gallery` in `@engine/cms-core/admin/groups`                                                                                                                                                                             |
| The owner's dashboard's gallery panels: visitors, search, chat, asks, antiques (`/admin/dashboard`, a tab per site)        | `cms/src/admin/dashboard/**` (`loaders/antiques.ts`, `asks.ts`, `visitors.ts` …, `view-body.jsx`, `panels-traffic.jsx`); the shop's panels and loaders are `@engine/cms-shop/admin/dashboard/*`, the shared SQL, periods, formats and copy `@engine/cms-core/admin/dashboard/*` |
| The leads inbox (`/admin/leads`) with its reply and source blocks — both sites' leads, filtered by `site`                  | `cms/src/admin/leads/**`; the shop's "Create partner" and a partner's leads are `@engine/cms-shop/admin/partners/*`                                                                                                                                                             |
| The seed screen (`/admin/seeds`: the gallery's sample and the catalogue) and its route                                     | `cms/src/admin/seeds/**`, `engine/apps/web/src/app/(payload)/admin/seeds/**`                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| "Draft from photos" — the AI cataloguing of a work, and its route                                                          | `cms/src/ai/**`, `engine/apps/web/src/app/(payload)/admin/ai/**`                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| The dashboard widget                                                                                                       | `cms/src/admin/widgets/**`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

The shop's own admin (`engine/apps/shop`) has none of these: its sidebar has no gallery section, its
Today card for leads opens the leads' list and its sales card the orders (no inbox or dashboard view
there yet — a follow-up once phase 25 makes them shared), and it shows no link to the wall.
