Look, Colour & Lettering

Three recommendations, and the reason behind each.

STYLE
Classic — but quiet, not ornate
Your maps are already decorated — a decorated interface competes with them. Mayfair can afford one: it sells furniture and ormolu, not paper.
COLOUR
Warm paper — not white, not dark
Cream
Brown
Green
Gold
Cream and brown are sampled straight from your logo. Warm grounds flatter aged paper better than bright white. Green marks links, gold marks prices.
LETTERING
Classic serif + plain sans
Spice Islands
Reading text stays plain and clear, even on a phone.
Classic serif for headings, plain sans for reading. We pick a Garamond descendant because it is the same lineage as the type printed on your maps.
