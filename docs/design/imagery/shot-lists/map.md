# Shot list — a map

For maps, sea charts and city plans. Set up as in the [gallery guide](../gallery-guide.md)
§4–§6: dark background, even light, colour card and ruler beside the sheet, camera
straight above. **No hands in any frame.** Name files `<stock>_<role>_<nn>` (the
[handover](../handover.md) §2).

| # | Shot | Role | Light | Needed | Get it right |
| - | ---- | ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------ |
| 1 | the grey board where the sheet will lie | `ref` | copy light | once per session | corners as bright as the middle |
| 2 | the whole front of the sheet | `recto` | copy light (both sides, or window + reflector) | **always** | every edge visible, a margin all round; card and ruler in frame; out of its mat and glass if it can come out |
| 3 | the whole back | `verso` | copy light | **always** | also when blank — "Verso: blank" is part of the record; atlas text on the back identifies the edition |
| 4 | the title cartouche | `detail` | copy light | **always** | the whole cartouche, closer than the recto |
| 5 | each defect in the condition notes — a tear, a repair, foxing, a stain, a trimmed margin | `detail` | copy light | **one per named defect** | the defect whole, with a little sound paper round it; never cleaned |
| 6 | the place the listing is about — the town, the island, the harbour | `detail` | copy light | should | the name legible |
| 7 | the colouring — a coloured border or area | `detail` | copy light | if coloured | at a scale where the brush strokes show |
| 8 | the scale bar, compass rose, imprint or publisher's line | `detail` | copy light | should | text readable at 100% |
| 9 | the plate mark at one corner | `detail` | copy light | if engraved | the embossed line and the margin beyond it |
| 10 | the whole sheet, lit from one side | `raking` | one lamp, 10–20° above the table, from the left | **always** | all other lights off; folds, plate mark and cockling show |
| 11 | the centre fold or any crease, close | `raking` | the same lamp; from the top if the fold runs across | if folded | the relief of the fold — it is not a flaw to hide |
| 12 | the watermark, and any tear or repair | `transmitted` | an LED light pad under the sheet, front lights off | better tier | loose sheets in sound condition only; ruler in frame |
| 13 | the map in its frame or mat, as it will be sent | `framed` | two lamps at 45° | if sold framed | no reflections in the glass; the whole moulding |
| 14 | the sheet beside the ruler and an A4 sheet | `scale` | copy light | optional | both fully visible |

## If the map is larger than your camera can take at 240 ppi

Photograph it whole anyway — that is the recto, and it is enough for the site (the
guide, §3). If it matters for Old East Indies prints, say so in the notes; a
sectioned shoot can follow.

## Before you send

- [ ] Recto, verso and at least one detail — the launch set.
- [ ] A detail for every defect the condition notes name.
- [ ] Card and ruler visible in every frame except the transmitted-light one.
- [ ] Every photo checked at full size: sharp in the corners, no edge cut off.
- [ ] The item's notes: size, defects, mat or frame, camera and light.
