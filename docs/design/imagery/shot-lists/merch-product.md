# Shot list — a shop product

*Bahasa Indonesia:* [merch-product.id.md](merch-product.id.md)

For Old East Indies products: framed and unframed prints, posters, postcards,
notebooks, coasters, totes and the gift wrap. Set up as in the
[shop guide](../shop-guide.md): catalogue shots on the plain background with the
colour card in frame; life shots in daylight with one reference frame per scene.
Name files `<product>_<role>_<nn>` ([handover](../handover.md) §2).

**Photograph the product, not every variant.** Take one set per product, plus one
`flat` for each choice that changes how it looks in the hand (a tote's colour, a
frame's finish). The configurator shows sizes, frames and mounts from its own layers
([room-scenes.md](../room-scenes.md)).

| # | Shot | Role | Light | Needed | Get it right |
| - | ---- | ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------ |
| 1 | the whole product, straight on | `flat` | even catalogue light | **always** | fills most of the frame; card in frame; this is the colour reference for the rest |
| 2 | one more `flat` per finish or colour the buyer chooses | `flat` | the same | if it has finishes | same framing as #1, so they compare |
| 3 | the paper or fabric, close — texture and print sharpness | `detail` | even light, or a low side light for texture | **always** | the Archive No. or the finest line of the image, sharp |
| 4 | a frame corner and joint; the mount's bevel | `detail` | even light | if framed | the moulding's finish true to colour |
| 5 | the back — hanging hardware, the label, a postcard's printed side | `detail` | even light | should | buyers ask how it hangs and what the back says |
| 6 | the product in a real room — on a wall, a desk, a table | `in-room` | daylight | **always** — a labelled mockup until a real photo exists | the product's colour matches #1 |
| 7 | in hands, in use — a postcard held, a tote on a shoulder, a print being hung | `lifestyle` | daylight: morning, late afternoon or open shade | should | faces only with consent |
| 8 | beside something everyone knows — a hand, a phone, an A4 sheet, a sofa | `scale` | daylight | should | the reference not closer to the camera than the product |
| 9 | wrapped in the gift cloth, and unwrapped; the gift note | `packaging` | daylight | if giftable | what the recipient opens |
| 10 | the parcel as it ships — tube or box, protection inside | `packaging` | daylight | should | the damage-free delivery promise, shown |

## The making — the owner agreed (interview question S14)

The making: the original on the copy stand, a print coming off the printer, a frame
being joined, a parcel being packed — hands at work, faces only with consent, as the
[shop guide](../shop-guide.md) §5 describes. Name them `showroom_making_<nn>` or
`<product>_lifestyle_<nn>`.

## Before you send

- [ ] `flat`, `detail` and `in-room` for every product — the launch set (requirement 7.12).
- [ ] The card in every catalogue shot; one reference frame at the start of each scene.
- [ ] Every life shot's product the same colour as its `flat`.
- [ ] No Hofker Bali Hotel poster as the lead image (D6).
- [ ] Consent noted for every recognisable face.
