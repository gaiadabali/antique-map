# Shot list — a print

For engravings, etchings, aquatints, lithographs and chromolithographs: views,
costume and natural-history plates, book illustrations. Set up as in the
[gallery guide](../gallery-guide.md) §4–§6. **No hands in any frame.** Name files
`<stock>_<role>_<nn>` ([handover](../handover.md) §2).

A collector of prints looks first at three things: the **plate mark** (it separates
an intaglio print from a later reproduction), the **lettering** under the image (it
names the artist, engraver and often the state), and the **paper**. The list follows
them.

| # | Shot | Role | Light | Needed | Get it right |
| - | ---- | ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------ |
| 1 | the grey board where the sheet will lie | `ref` | copy light | once per session | corners as bright as the middle |
| 2 | the whole front of the sheet, margins included | `recto` | copy light | **always** | every edge visible; card and ruler in frame; out of its mat if it can come out |
| 3 | the whole back | `verso` | copy light | **always** | a book plate's text or page number on the back identifies the book and edition |
| 4 | the lettering below the image — title, "del." and "sculp." lines, publisher | `detail` | copy light | **always**, if lettered | every word readable at 100% |
| 5 | the plate mark at one corner | `detail` | copy light | if intaglio | the embossed line with the margin beyond |
| 6 | the heart of the image — a figure, a building, a face | `detail` | copy light | should | the engraved or drawn line resolves as lines |
| 7 | the colouring | `detail` | copy light | if coloured | brush strokes or printed colour visible — they tell hand colour from printed |
| 8 | each defect in the condition notes | `detail` | copy light | **one per named defect** | the defect whole; never cleaned |
| 9 | the whole sheet, lit from one side | `raking` | one lamp, 10–20° above the table, from the left | **always** | other lights off; the plate mark's embossing and any folds show |
| 10 | the plate mark and the lettering, close | `raking` | the same lamp | if intaglio | the relief of the plate edge |
| 11 | the watermark and chain lines | `transmitted` | an LED light pad under the sheet, front lights off | better tier | loose sheets in sound condition only; ruler in frame |
| 12 | the print in its frame or mat, as it will be sent | `framed` | two lamps at 45° | if sold framed | no reflections; the whole moulding |
| 13 | the sheet beside the ruler and an A4 sheet | `scale` | copy light | optional | both fully visible |

## Prints from books

A plate taken from a book often has text on the back, a page or plate number, and a
fold or stub where it was bound. Photograph all three — they are how a collector
identifies the source work (CONTENT-MODEL.md §1, `publication`).

## Before you send

- [ ] Recto, verso and at least one detail — the launch set.
- [ ] The lettering readable, the plate mark shown, if the print has them.
- [ ] A detail for every defect the condition notes name.
- [ ] Card and ruler in every frame except the transmitted-light one.
- [ ] The item's notes: size, defects, mat or frame, camera and light.
