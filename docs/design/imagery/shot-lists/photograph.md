# Shot list — a photograph

*Bahasa Indonesia:* [photograph.id.md](photograph.id.md)

For albumen prints, gelatin silver prints and other historic photographs, loose or on
their original mounts. Set up as in the [gallery guide](../gallery-guide.md) §4–§6,
with three differences:

- **Photographs are the most light-sensitive things in the gallery.** Never sun, never
  a hot lamp; keep the light on only while you shoot.
- **Never turn a photo into black and white.** The warm brown of an albumen print, the
  cool or olive tone of a silver print, the fading at the edges — all of it is
  information. The colour card stays in every frame.
- **Glossy surfaces reflect.** Two lamps at 45° usually avoid it; polarising filters
  (best tier) remove it.

**No hands in any frame.** Name files `<stock>_<role>_<nn>`
([handover](../handover.md) §2).

| # | Shot | Role | Light | Needed | Get it right |
| - | ---- | ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------ |
| 1 | the grey board where the photo will lie | `ref` | copy light | once per session | corners as bright as the middle |
| 2 | the whole front — the print **and its mount** | `recto` | copy light | **always** | the mount's edges visible; card and ruler in frame |
| 3 | the whole back of the mount (or of the print) | `verso` | copy light | **always** | studio stamps, inscriptions, album remnants — often the best evidence of who made it |
| 4 | the studio's name or blind stamp on the mount | `detail` | copy light, or a low side light for a blind stamp | if present | readable; a blind stamp may need the raking shot instead |
| 5 | a caption, number or signature in the image | `detail` | copy light | if present | readable at 100% |
| 6 | the heart of the image — a face, a building, a street | `detail` | copy light | should | the finest detail the print holds |
| 7 | the tones — a bright sky and a deep shadow together | `detail` | copy light | should | no clipped highlight; shadow detail visible |
| 8 | each defect in the condition notes — fading, silvering, cracks, foxing on the mount, a tear, a crease | `detail` | copy light | **one per named defect** | the defect whole; never cleaned |
| 9 | the whole print, lit from one side | `raking` | one lamp, 10–20° above the table, from the left — gently, briefly | **always** | cracks in the surface, a cockled mount, creases show |
| 10 | silvering or a surface sheen, if the notes mention it | `detail` | one lamp placed so its reflection falls on the area | if present | the mirror-like sheen visible — it is part of the condition |
| 11 | the photo in its frame, as it will be sent | `framed` | two lamps at 45° | if sold framed | no reflections |
| 12 | the photo beside the ruler and an A4 sheet | `scale` | copy light | optional | both fully visible |

**No transmitted-light shot.** A photograph on a mount must never be lit from behind,
and a loose one gains nothing from it.

## Albums

An album is photographed like a book: the cover, the spine, and each opening that
matters, lying as it opens naturally — never pressed flat. A dedicated shot list for
books and albums is a follow-up (README.md).

## Before you send

- [ ] Recto (with its mount), verso and at least one detail — the launch set.
- [ ] The photo left in colour, with the card in every frame.
- [ ] A detail for every defect the condition notes name.
- [ ] Studio stamps and inscriptions readable.
- [ ] The item's notes: size of print and mount, defects, frame, camera and light.
