# Questions for the owner — Indies Gallery and Old East Indies

**For:** the owner of Indies Gallery and Old East Indies · **From:** the design team ·
**Prepared:** 1 October 2026 · **Status:** not yet sent (TASKS.md OA2, 6.1.b)

We are designing both new sites. Most of what we need we already have from your
current site, your notes of 11 September and our research. What is left below are
things **only you can tell us**. There are 15 questions for the gallery and 15 for the
shop, **most important first**. If you only have twenty minutes, answer the first five
of each.

How to answer: whatever is quickest. Short answers, a voice note, or a WhatsApp
message are all fine. "I don't know yet" and "you decide" are good answers too. If you
skip a question, we go ahead with the default written under it, and you can change it
later.

Questions that need a lawyer or an accountant (your companies, tax, export clearance,
the returns wording, Hofker's rights) are **not** here. We track those separately.

---

## Indies Gallery — the originals

### G1. What name should the gallery's new site go by?
Today the site shows three names on one page: "Indies Gallery", "Antique Maps
Indonesia" and, in the footer, "Indies Gallery, based in Singapore".
**Why we ask:** the name sits on every page, every email, every certificate and every
Google result. Changing it after launch costs search traffic.
**If we don't hear back:** "Indies Gallery" is the name, and antiquemapsindonesia.com
stays the web address.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Positioning.*

### G2. Where are the originals kept, and where can a buyer come to see one?
For example: some in Singapore, some in Jakarta, some in Bali? By appointment only?
**Why we ask:** where a map sits decides which company sells it, which currency it is
priced in, whether it may leave Indonesia, and which city a buyer can view it in.
**If we don't hear back:** stock is in Singapore and Jakarta; viewings are by
appointment in those two cities only.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Positioning, Capabilities (stock locations).*

### G3. How do your sales actually happen today?
Roughly: how many buyers pay online without talking to you first, how many start on
WhatsApp, by email, or in person? Which kind of buyer spends the most — collectors,
museums and libraries, designers and hotels, or families looking for their town?
**Why we ask:** it decides what the big button on each item says — "Buy" or "Ask
about this piece" — and which buyers the home page speaks to first.
**If we don't hear back:** a "Buy" button up to about USD 25,000 and a conversation
above that; collectors first.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Users, Operating Context.*

### G4. Which prices do you show in public, and from what value do you say "Price on request"?
The current site shows prices up to USD 38,800 and "On Request" for some pieces.
**Why we ask:** a shown price lets a collector buy at night without waiting for you; a
hidden one starts a conversation. We need your line.
**If we don't hear back:** every priced item shows its price; "Price on request" only
where you already use it today.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Capabilities (price tiers).*

### G5. How long will you hold a piece for a buyer — and for a museum waiting on paperwork?
We planned 48 hours for a normal reserve. A museum asking for a proforma invoice may
need weeks. Should a proforma request hold the pieces straight away, or only once your
team has approved it?
**Why we ask:** an automatic hold is convenient, but anyone could keep a one-of-one map
off the market by asking for proformas again and again.
**If we don't hear back:** reserve for 48 hours; a proforma holds its pieces only after
your team approves it, until the invoice's due date.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Capabilities (holds, proformas); journey J-G2.*

### G6. What do you promise a buyer if something is wrong?
Do you guarantee authenticity for life? Can a buyer return a piece — within how many
days, and who pays the shipping back? Your lawyer will word it; we need your intention.
**Why we ask:** this is the first thing a USD 5,000 buyer looks for, and today's site
has no terms at all.
**If we don't hear back:** nothing is promised on the site until your lawyer's wording
arrives; the space for it is designed and waiting.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Brand Commitments.*

### G7. What exactly does Dr Parry's certificate cover?
Does every piece get one — maps, prints, photographs, books? Is each one signed? May
we show a sample certificate on the site?
**Why we ask:** the certificate is your strongest trust signal and no competitor has
it. We want to state it exactly, not loosely.
**If we don't hear back:** every original comes with a certificate; no sample is shown
until Dr Parry agrees.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Brand Commitments, Evidence.*

### G8. Do you want buyers to be able to make an offer online?
If yes: on which pieces, and is there a level below which an offer is politely declined
automatically (for example, below 80% of the price)?
**Why we ask:** offers bring in buyers who would never ask, but every offer lands on
your team's desk.
**If we don't hear back:** offers on pieces between about USD 5,000 and 25,000; no
automatic decline — your team sees every offer.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Capabilities (offers); journey J-G6.*

### G9. How fast can your team reply, and in which hours?
To a price request, a WhatsApp message, a request for a proforma. Which time zone do
you work in?
**Why we ask:** the site will promise a reply time next to every question button. A
promise we break costs more than no promise.
**If we don't hear back:** "We reply within two working days" (the line in your design
draft), Singapore time.
*Feeds: platform PRODUCT.md › Operating Context; journeys J-G1, J-G2.*

### G10. May we name the museums and libraries that have bought from you?
Your notes list the National Museum of Singapore, the National Library of Australia,
the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Leiden University. Is the National Library of Singapore also
a client? Do you have their permission to be named?
**Why we ask:** a named institution is powerful proof, but only with permission.
**If we don't hear back:** the list is shown only once you confirm that permission;
until then the Institutions page explains how institutions buy.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Users, Evidence.*

### G11. How do you ship an original, and who pays the import duties?
Which courier, how is it packed, is it insured, and does the buyer pay duties on
arrival?
**Why we ask:** a buyer sees the shipping cost before paying; a surprise at the door
loses the next sale.
**If we don't hear back:** shipping for valuable pieces is quoted by your team and
insured separately; duties are paid by the buyer on arrival.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Capabilities (shipping); journeys J-G2, J-G4.*

### G12. Can you send us a report from Google Analytics for the current site?
The current site already has Google Analytics. A download of the last 12 months —
countries, phones against computers, the most-visited pages — is enough. We will not
log in to your site ourselves.
**Why we ask:** it tells us which screens most buyers use, so we design those first.
**If we don't hear back:** phones first, with most visitors landing on an item page
from Google.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Users.*

### G13. Which facts about the gallery may we state?
For example: the year you started (your design draft says 2001 and "25 years"), the
number of pieces you hold (the site says over 9,500), memberships (such as IMCoS),
press coverage, fairs.
**Why we ask:** every fact on the site must be true; we will not publish a number you
have not confirmed.
**If we don't hear back:** only "over 9,500 authentic antiques", as today's site says,
and the certificate.
*Feeds: gallery PRODUCT.md › Product Purpose, Evidence.*

### G14. When a designer downloads an item sheet for a client, should it show your price?
Designers and hotels often show a piece to their client before buying.
**Why we ask:** some designers add their own margin and prefer a sheet without a price;
others need the price to get approval.
**If we don't hear back:** the sheet shows the same price or "Price on request" as the
web page, with the date it was printed.
*Feeds: journey J-G3.*

### G15. Who on your teams will use the new admin every day, and in which language? *(both sites)*
Who catalogues, who answers buyers, who packs orders, who runs the shop and the
showroom — and are they more comfortable in English or Indonesian?
**Why we ask:** the admin is half of this project. We will watch two of them work
(about an hour each) and design around them.
**If we don't hear back:** the admin opens in Indonesian for shop staff and English for
gallery staff; anyone can switch.
*Feeds: platform PRODUCT.md › Users.*

## Old East Indies — the shop

### S1. What will the shop sell on day one, and who makes it?
Which products, sizes, papers and frames — and are they printed and framed in your own
workshop in Bali, by a local printer, or both? Your "Katalog Bali 2026" PDF would
answer most of this; we could not open the copy we received — could you send it again?
**Why we ask:** the product options are the heart of the shop page. We cannot design
them from guesses.
**If we don't hear back:** a sample range (posters, giclée prints, framed prints,
postcards, notebooks, totes) is designed as a placeholder and nothing is published.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Product Purpose, Capabilities.*

### S2. What are your retail prices?
A price list per product and size is ideal; a few examples help too.
**Why we ask:** prices decide how the "from postcard to framed print" ladder looks and
which payment methods appear.
**If we don't hear back:** test prices only on the staging site; no price is invented
for the live site.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Capabilities (pricing).*

### S3. Do you send orders abroad today — where to, and who pays the import duties?
For example a tourist who buys in Bali and wants it sent home to the Netherlands.
**Why we ask:** it decides whether shoppers abroad can check out at all, in which
currency, and what they are told about duties.
**If we don't hear back:** we ship abroad from Bali by courier; the recipient pays any
duties on arrival, and the site says so before payment.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Positioning, Capabilities; journeys J-S2, J-S5.*

### S4. Tell us about the showroom.
Opening hours, what is on the shelves, whether visitors can pay by QRIS there, whether
online orders can be collected there, and whether we may put QR labels on the walls.
**Why we ask:** the showroom and the site are one shop. A visitor scanning a label
should be able to buy the piece in front of them.
**If we don't hear back:** no hours are published — the page says "Message us on
WhatsApp before you visit"; pickup and QR labels are designed and switched on when you
confirm.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Operating Context; journey J-S4.*

### S5. What does a partner shop, hotel or company get from you today?
Discount level, minimum order, how they pay, how you deliver — and which kinds of
business you want to attract most.
**Why we ask:** the Partnership page must say clearly what a business gets before it
applies.
**If we don't hear back:** one trade price level and a minimum order, set by you in the
admin; orders by quote, paid by bank transfer or payment link.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Users, Capabilities (partners); journey J-S3.*

### S6. Which WhatsApp number do shoppers message, who answers, and when?
**Why we ask:** "Ask on WhatsApp" sits on every product. The site will say when a reply
can be expected, so we need the real hours.
**If we don't hear back:** the showroom's WhatsApp Business number, no reply hours
promised, and an automatic reply outside working hours.
*Feeds: platform PRODUCT.md › Operating Context; every shop journey.*

### S7. Which products are kept in stock, and how long do made-to-order pieces take?
**Why we ask:** the site promises "ready today" or "ships in N days" only when it is
true. A wrong promise is the fastest way to lose a gift buyer.
**If we don't hear back:** everything framed is made to order; no number of days is
promised until you give us one.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Capabilities; journeys J-S1, J-S2, J-S5.*

### S8. Which artwork should lead the shop?
The Bali Hotel posters are well known, but Hofker's rights are not yet confirmed.
Which other artworks or collections would you put first?
**Why we ask:** the first image on the home page sets the whole mood. It must work even
if the Hofker line cannot launch.
**If we don't hear back:** the home page opens on a map of the archipelago, and the
Hofker line waits for its licence.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Brand Commitments.*

### S9. Where do your buyers come from today, and how do they order?
Instagram messages, WhatsApp, walking into the showroom, through partner shops? An
export of Instagram Insights (followers' countries, ages) would help a lot.
**Why we ask:** it tells us which phone screens and which languages come first.
**If we don't hear back:** Instagram on a phone first, in English and Indonesian.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Users, Operating Context.*

### S10. Do you offer gift wrapping, gift notes or gift cards?
Your plans mention a wrapping cloth printed with an old map. Does it exist, and what
does it cost?
**Why we ask:** gifts are a large part of the shop; each option is its own choice on
the product page and in checkout.
**If we don't hear back:** a gift note and "hide the prices" on the packing slip only;
wrapping is added when you confirm it.
*Feeds: journey J-S5.*

### S11. Can you deliver to a hotel or villa in Bali before a tourist flies home?
And same-day delivery in Denpasar or Jakarta?
**Why we ask:** a tourist who cannot carry a framed print home still wants to buy it
here.
**If we don't hear back:** delivery within Bali by courier with a "deliver before" date;
same-day where the courier offers it.
*Feeds: journey J-S2.*

### S12. What happens if a print arrives damaged, or a buyer changes their mind?
Our plan says a damaged print is replaced after the buyer sends a photo. Is that your
promise? Your lawyer will word the returns policy; we need your intention.
**Why we ask:** a clear promise next to the "Add to bag" button removes the last doubt.
**If we don't hear back:** a damaged print is replaced on a photo; nothing else is
promised until the lawyer's wording arrives.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Evidence (absences); journeys J-S1, J-S5.*

### S13. Which offers do you want: free shipping above an amount, a welcome discount, vouchers?
Your design draft says "Free shipping in Indonesia over Rp 500.000".
**Why we ask:** the bag shows how close a shopper is to free shipping, but only if the
offer is real.
**If we don't hear back:** no free-shipping line and no welcome discount until you set
them in the admin.
*Feeds: journeys J-S1, J-S6.*

### S14. May we film or photograph how the prints are made?
You asked for a site that shows how souvenirs are made from the originals. Where does
that happen, and could it be part of the photo day in the showroom?
**Why we ask:** "how we make them" is the story no print-on-demand shop can tell.
**If we don't hear back:** the story is told in words and still photos from the photo
day.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Product Purpose; the photo shoot (TASKS.md 6.2).*

### S15. How should the shop speak?
Casual "kamu" or polite "Anda" in Indonesian? And the name "Old East Indies" recalls
the colonial era: our plan is to celebrate the maps and the islands, never the VOC
(no VOC logo, no colonial nostalgia). Is that how you see it?
**Why we ask:** the tone runs through every button, email and WhatsApp message.
**If we don't hear back:** "kamu", a warm and playful tone, and no VOC imagery.
*Feeds: shop PRODUCT.md › Brand Commitments; voice (TASKS.md 6.3).*
