# Experience — Old East Indies

How the merch shop behaves. The research is in RESEARCH.md §3; the visual world
is chosen in the Design stage and recorded in `engine/apps/emporium/DESIGN.md`.

**Feeling to aim for:** the optimism of a 1930s steamship-line brochure, retold
for today's Bali café crowd — warm, sunlit, giftable, a little playful — with the
archive's credibility underneath: every product carries its **Archive No.** and
the story of the original it comes from. Warmer and louder than the gallery; the
same family.

**Research evidence — not a pinned direction.** The benchmark proposed "Hotel
Bali 1928" (Art Deco travel poster: Limelight + Jost; Lagoon `#1F6F78`, Hotel
Cream `#F4E7CC`, Frangipani Coral `#E4704F`, Sun Ochre `#E8A93A`, Palm Green
`#2F5B3B`, Night Navy `#1B2940`), "Chart Room" (IM Fell English + IBM Plex) and
"Nusantara Pop" (Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Serif, batik-derived tiles with
a credited batik artist). The Deco travel poster is also the stock look of every
vintage-poster print-on-demand shop — and a 1930s steamship brochure is colonial
tourism promotion, in tension with this brand's own commitment below. The Design stage
therefore runs impeccable's full direction round (TASKS.md 12.2) with **each
research direction entering as at most one candidate**, a **cultural review by
Indonesian designers and buyers**, and a written position on what the chosen
world celebrates (the archipelago, the craft of printmaking) and what it avoids
(the colonial gaze). Nothing above binds the outcome — and since 2026-09-28 the
shop shares the gallery's base system and differs in its accents (D9's shape,
see EXPERIENCE-GALLERY.md); the cultural review applies to those accents; the typewriter-style Archive
No. tag shared with the gallery is the one sister element already agreed.

**Two cautions carried into the design:**
- **Hofker is likely in copyright until 2051** (COMPLIANCE.md §8). The Bali Hotel
  hero line launches only once the licence or estate position is confirmed; the
  design must work if the hero is a different artist.
- Celebrate cartography and the archipelago, **not the VOC** — no VOC logo, no
  colonial nostalgia as a brand voice.

---

## 1. Who it serves

Tourists in Bali and Jakarta buying something packable with meaning; expats
furnishing homes; the Indonesian diaspora and the Dutch-Indisch community;
gift buyers (oleh-oleh, housewarming, Lebaran, Imlek, Christmas, Sinterklaas);
hotels, villas, cafés and companies buying art and gifts in quantity. Most
arrive from Instagram on a phone.

## 2. Information architecture

**Top navigation:** Shop · Collections · Places & Eras · Gifts · Stories · For
Business · **Partnership** (the retailer programme) · Visit the Showroom.
**Utility bar:** ship-to/currency · language · WhatsApp · bag — no shopper account: shoppers buy as guests and track
orders by number (D31, TASKS.md 28.5).

**Shop** mega-menu, three columns plus a feature tile (the hero line):
- **Wall Art** — Posters · Giclée · Framed · Canvas · Murals · Print from the
  Archive · Frames & mounts
- **Paper & Gifts** — Postcards & sets · Cards · Notebooks · Calendars ·
  Stickers · Puzzles · Gift sets · Gift cards
- **Home & Wear** — Totes · Tees · Mugs & coasters · Tea towels · Cushions ·
  Phone cases

**Collections** (story-led): the hero line · Old Bali · Batavia/Jakarta · Spice
Islands · Ships & Steamers · Flora & Fauna · Temples · People & Costume · Kids'
Archipelago; plus **Artists & Mapmakers** (Hofker, Nieuwenkamp, Tyra Kleen,
Kassian Céphas, Blaeu…).

**Places & Eras:** Bali, Java, Sumatra, Borneo, Sulawesi, Maluku, Nusa
Tenggara, Papua, Singapore/Malaya · before 1700 · 1700s · 1800s · 1900–1942 —
the same gazetteer as the gallery.

**Gifts:** by price (under Rp 150k / 500k / 1.5m; under €25 / €50 for export
markets) · by recipient · by occasion (oleh-oleh, housewarming, wedding, Lebaran,
Imlek, Christmas, Sinterklaas, corporate).

**Filters** (bottom sheet on mobile): type · format · size range · orientation ·
dominant colour · place · era · subject · maker · room · mood · price ·
personalisable · availability (in the showroom · ships today · made to order).
**Sort:** featured · best-selling · new · price · date of the original.

**Tiles:** a second, in-room image on hover/swipe · maker and year · "From"
price (the cheapest variant that can ship to this destination) · frame swatches
· one badge · quick-add for items without options · the **Reproduction** label.

## 4. The product page

Top to bottom, mobile first:

1. **Images** — framed on a wall first, then the flat artwork, a deep zoom of the
   scan (the detail is the selling point), close-ups, a scale shot, the gift
   wrap. AR "view on your wall" is v2.
2. **Title block** — "Bali Hotel, Denpasar — Giclée print", the artist or
   mapmaker and year, the **Archive No.**, the **Reproduction** label, one badge
   (Hero · Printed in Bali · In the showroom now · Limited edition), and a price
   that reads "From Rp 95.000" until options are chosen, then the live price.
3. **The configurator** (§5).
4. **Delivery promise** for the ship-to destination: "Ready at the showroom in
   2 hours" · "Made to order, ships in 3–5 days" · duties included or estimated
   for export — read against a **holiday calendar** (Nyepi closes Bali — showroom,
   couriers and airport; Lebaran stops couriers nationwide) that also drives a
   homepage announcement. A **shipping estimate** for the saved district appears
   here, because a shipping cost first seen at checkout drives abandonment; and
   the cash-on-delivery position is stated plainly ("No COD — pay by QRIS or VA,
   confirmed instantly").
5. **Sticky buy bar** — Add to bag (with price) · "Ask on WhatsApp" (product and
   chosen options prefilled) · gift-wrap toggle.
6. **Trust row** — paper and ink details, payment icons for this destination
   (QRIS, e-wallets, bank transfer, cards, PayPal), the returns rule as counsel
   words it, a free reprint if it arrives damaged.
7. **The story** — 100–150 words and a "where is this?" map pin, linking to the
   full article.
8. **The original** — from the sister link (BRANDS.md §5): "The original is at
   Indies Gallery" with its price **in the visitor's market currency** (IDR for an
   Indonesian destination — the rupiah rule applies here too) or "Enquire about the
   original"; when sold, "Made from our scan of the original, now in a private
   collection" with similar originals. The link respects export status: a
   `domestic-only` original seen from abroad offers no buy route. The link says
   what it is — "Indies Gallery is our sister gallery: a separate shop with its
   own account".
9. Specifications (collapsed).
10. **More with this image** — the same design on other products, a matching
    frame, a wall set.
11. Reviews and customer photos — **hidden while there are none** (an empty band
    is omitted, never shown empty).

**"You're in the showroom."** A product page opened from a showroom QR label
switches to a light, weak-Wi-Fi-friendly mode: pick it up now, buy it here and
take it, or buy it here and have it sent home.

## 5. The configurator

| Step | Options | Rules |
| ---- | ------- | ----- |
| Format | Poster · Giclée · Framed · Canvas (murals are a v2 tool) | option cards each with a "from" price |
| Size | Posters 21×30 · 30×40 · 50×70 · 70×100 cm (standard frames); giclée at true proportions, long edge 30/45/60/90 cm | proportions locked; **max size from the scan's resolution** (≥ 240 ppi); editions never larger than the original |
| Paper (giclée) | smooth cotton rag ~308 gsm · textured etching ~310 gsm | close-up swatch; a paper sample kit product |
| Frame | none · slim black · natural teak · dark teak · antique gold | shows frame width and total outer size |
| Mount | none / 4 / 6 / 8 cm; warm white · ivory · black | only with a frame; never on canvas |
| Glazing | acrylic (default) · UV anti-reflective acrylic · glass | glass only for pickup or delivery within Bali |
| Personalise (v2) | caption ≤ 40 characters; map pin + a two-line title panel | brand font fixed; preview before ordering |
| Gift | wrapping cloth printed with an archive map · gift note · hide prices | each a priced line |

**The options are the page** — server-rendered radio groups in a GET form that
works without JavaScript, hydrated progressively; only the preview loads on intent
(DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §7). The destination's price table ships with the page, so the
price updates instantly on the client (display only — the bag re-prices on the
server).

**Preview behaviour** — layered in the browser from a pre-sized ~1200 px AVIF,
9-slice frame layers, pre-composited room plates and an SVG scale view, updating
in under 100 ms without a reload on the reference device. Three views: **Flat**,
**On a wall** (three wall colours), **To scale** (a 1.7 m person and a 2 m sofa
with dimension lines). On a phone the preview stays pinned above a pull-up options
panel. The chosen options live in the URL, so a configuration can be sent on
WhatsApp or turned into a business quote ("Turn this into a quote" → the `Quote`
surface). Impossible combinations are disabled **with the reason shown**. The
preview is decoration over a text summary of the selection (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §9);
the price change is announced politely; swatches carry names and ≥ 24 px targets.

**Pricing** — one price table per product family shared by all artworks, times
the artwork multiplier; separate market price lists (IDR for Indonesia;
USD/EUR/AUD/SGD built from production + duties-paid shipping) rounded to clean
price points, tax included (COMMERCE.md §3). Bundles: print + frame, wall sets
10–15% off, "3 for 2" cards.

## 6. Design pages — one image, many products

`/designs/{slug}`: the artwork large, its story and Archive No., the original's
status at the gallery, and **every product made from it** — print, poster,
postcard, tote, notebook, mug — as the Society6 / Rifle "shop by pattern" model
does. It is also the landing page for "Print from the Archive": any gallery scan
with rights and resolution becomes a configurable print with no stock.

## 7. Bag and checkout

**Bag drawer:** line items with their configuration · a **free-shipping progress
bar** · a frame upsell for unframed prints · postcard add-ons · the **voucher
field** Indonesian shoppers expect · the delivery estimate for the destination.

**Bag edge cases are designed, not discovered:** a line that becomes invalid
after a ship-to change (glass glazing is Bali-only) says why and offers acrylic;
a showroom item that sells out while in the bag; mixed fulfilment (stocked +
made-to-order + pickup) shows an estimate per shipment; every voucher failure
(expired, minimum spend, not combinable) is worded to instruct; the free-shipping
bar re-computes after a currency change; gift wrap is per line or per order, and
says which.

**Checkout — Indonesian destination:** WhatsApp number first (order updates
arrive there), normalised (`08xx` → `+62 8xx`) with a "this is my WhatsApp"
toggle · **one "Full name" field** (many Indonesians have one name) · address by
searchable pickers (province → city → district → sub-district), postcode filled
from the sub-district, **map pin optional** · couriers with price and ETA (JNE,
J&T, SiCepat; GoSend/GrabExpress same-day in Denpasar and Jakarta) · **showroom
pickup** · voucher · QRIS, e-wallets, virtual accounts that confirm automatically
(never "upload your transfer receipt"), cards, pay-later, Alfamart/Indomaret —
filtered by amount (PAYMENTS.md §3) · prices in **IDR only**.

**"I'm visiting Bali."** A tourist on a Bali IP gets three clear paths instead of
Indonesian defaults: deliver to my hotel or villa **before my departure date**,
collect at the showroom, or send it home (switching ship-to and currency).
Packaging copy says what fits a suitcase ("packs flat, under 1 kg").

**Checkout — export destination:** guest checkout · express wallets where the
seller supports them · a shipping estimate and the market currency from the
ship-to selector · duties included or clearly estimated.

**Payment pending** is a designed page (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §2): amount, VA number
with copy, per-bank steps, countdown, the bank-cap warning, "we'll WhatsApp you
when it's paid", automatic switch to paid; QRIS with "save QR to gallery" and
e-wallet deep links, because a phone cannot scan its own screen; failures retry
with another method and keep the bag. In an **in-app browser** (Instagram,
WhatsApp, TikTok) a payment step that cannot complete says so and offers "open in
your browser".

**Gifts:** a recipient address separate from the buyer's, a preview of the gift
note, a packing slip with prices hidden, a target delivery date (before Lebaran),
digital gift cards sent to the recipient by email or WhatsApp on a chosen date,
and a balance-check page.

**After the order:** guests track by order number plus email or WhatsApp number
(`OrderLookup`), with the courier timeline and the tracking link in every
WhatsApp update; pickups get a code or QR, a "ready" notice, hours, a map and who
may collect. A print that arrives damaged is replaced on a photo claim.

## 8. The showroom, Instagram and WhatsApp

- **The showroom is a stock location**: "In the showroom now" badge and filter,
  click & collect, and (v2) buy in the showroom and ship home, printed near the
  buyer with duties paid. QR codes on the walls open each product page.
- **Instagram:** an `/ig` page on the shop's own domain replaces the Linktree —
  recent posts, each linked to its products. One Meta catalogue feed drives
  Instagram product tags **and** the WhatsApp catalogue, so the Drive PDFs can
  go. Checkout always happens on the site (Permendag 31/2023: social media may
  promote, not take payment).
- **WhatsApp** is a first-class channel: "Ask on WhatsApp" on every product with
  context prefilled **in the page's language** (from the brand lexicon), a stated
  reply window ("replies 09–21 WITA") with an after-hours auto-reply, order and
  shipping updates, and B2B quotes. A payment link a staff member sends on
  WhatsApp opens a designed `Pay` page — item, terms, expiry, the seller's
  identity — never a bare gateway screen.

## 9. For Business

**Retail partners are separate from For Business** (D31). The shop already
supplies 100+ shops, so a **Partnership** page — reached from a highlight in the
home hero and from the header — explains the programme and ends in the sign-up /
sign-in section. Retailers are the **only** accounts on this site: an
application approved by staff, then a retailer area with the trade terms (D32)
and orders by quote (TASKS.md 28.5).

Hotels, villas, cafés, embassies and companies: an enquiry flow (quantities,
sizes, framing, deadlines) that becomes a **quote page** (lines, validity, PDF,
accept → payment link — the `Quote` surface), reachable too from any configured
product ("Turn this into a quote"); corporate
gift sets; custom sizes and murals by quote. v2: a trade portal with price tiers,
quotes and invoices. The research's standout opportunity: the Bali Hotel in the
posters still operates as Inna Bali in central Denpasar — a co-branded
collection, a lobby corner, room art with QR story cards.

## 11. Scope

**MVP (launch):** the site with the `/ig` page (posts curated in the CMS);
redirects for every old Squarespace URL, and the gallery's item pages linking to
the exact prints made from each work (MIGRATION.md §10); 150–300 products
with size/frame options; the configurator with live preview, scale view and live
price; the story and "own the original" blocks; collections, filters, gifts;
gift wrap, notes and digital gift cards; the bag drawer with progress bar,
upsells and vouchers; Midtrans methods for Indonesia, cards and PayPal for
export; currency by destination; courier rates, same-day and showroom pickup;
WhatsApp chat, and order updates on WhatsApp once the provider is chosen (D14 —
by email until then); the showroom as a stock location; the Meta
catalogue feed; welcome offer; reviews; the budgets.

**v2:** a Singapore seller for export with Apple/Google Pay and duties-paid
delivery; automatic routing to Prodigi/Gelato and the full "Print from the
Archive" range; AR view on your wall; gallery-wall sets and builder; personalised
old maps of Indonesian towns; loyalty and referrals; a showroom till on the same
stock (QRIS); marketplace sync through an omnichannel hub (Jubelio/Ginee); the
trade portal; Instagram customer photos; WhatsApp broadcasts.

**Later:** made-to-measure murals; heritage room packs for hotels; a corporate
gift builder; numbered editions with a certificate or NFC tag; a Dutch store;
"find your family's town"; postcard or calendar subscriptions.

**Gaps nobody owns** (RESEARCH.md §3.9): Indonesian archive imagery **with
provenance** at lifestyle prices; personalised old maps of Indonesian towns;
buy in Bali, delivered home duties paid; hotels and heritage; packable souvenirs
under 1 kg with a story card; the Indisch community in the Netherlands (Dutch
stories, the Tong Tong Fair).
