# Experience — Old East Indies

How the merch shop behaves. The research is in RESEARCH.md §3; the visual world
is chosen in the Design stage and recorded in `engine/apps/emporium/DESIGN.md`.

**Feeling to aim for:** the optimism of a 1930s steamship-line brochure, retold
for today's Bali café crowd — warm, sunlit, giftable, a little playful — with the
archive's credibility underneath: every product carries its **Archive No.** and
the story of the original it comes from. Warmer and louder than the gallery; the
same family.

**Research evidence — not a pinned direction.** The benchmark proposed "Hotel
Bali 1928" (Art Deco travel poster: Limelight + Jost; Lagoon `#1F6F78`, Hotel
Cream `#F4E7CC`, Frangipani Coral `#E4704F`, Sun Ochre `#E8A93A`, Palm Green
`#2F5B3B`, Night Navy `#1B2940`), "Chart Room" (IM Fell English + IBM Plex) and
"Nusantara Pop" (Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Serif, batik-derived tiles with
a credited batik artist). The Deco travel poster is also the stock look of every
vintage-poster print-on-demand shop — and a 1930s steamship brochure is colonial
tourism promotion, in tension with this brand's own commitment below. The Design stage
therefore runs impeccable's full direction round (TASKS.md 12.2) with **each
research direction entering as at most one candidate**, a **cultural review by
Indonesian designers and buyers**, and a written position on what the chosen
world celebrates (the archipelago, the craft of printmaking) and what it avoids
(the colonial gaze). Nothing above binds the outcome — and since 2026-09-28 the
shop shares the gallery's whole base system — layout, components, buttons and type,
the typewriter-style Archive No. tag among its components — and differs only in its
accents, palette and signature details (D9's shape, see EXPERIENCE-GALLERY.md); the
cultural review applies to those accents.

**Two cautions carried into the design:**
- **Hofker is likely in copyright until 2051** (COMPLIANCE.md §8). The Bali Hotel
  hero line launches only once the licence or estate position is confirmed; the
  design must work if the hero is a different artist.
- Celebrate cartography and the archipelago, **not the VOC** — no VOC logo, no
  colonial nostalgia as a brand voice.

## 1. Who it serves

Tourists in Bali and Jakarta buying something packable with meaning; expats
furnishing homes; gift buyers (oleh-oleh, housewarming, Lebaran, Imlek,
Christmas, Sinterklaas); hotels, villas, cafés and companies buying art and gifts
in quantity.
**Walk-ins at the Denpasar showroom are the main buyers today** (S9), so the
showroom's QR labels and pickup come first among the journeys; most of the rest
arrive from Instagram on a phone.

**At launch the shop sells within Indonesia only, and only what it has in stock**
(the owner's answers S3 and S7, 2026-10-01). A tourist buys for delivery to a Bali
hotel or villa before departure, or collects at the showroom; nothing is shipped
abroad yet, so the Indonesian diaspora and the Dutch-Indisch community abroad wait
for export (§11) — they can still message the shop on WhatsApp.

5. **Sticky buy bar** — Add to bag (with price) · "Ask on WhatsApp" (product and

## 5. The configurator

| Step | Options | Rules |
| ---- | ------- | ----- |
| Format | Poster · Giclée · Framed · Canvas (murals are a v2 tool) | option cards each with a "from" price |
| Size | Posters 21×30 · 30×40 · 50×70 · 70×100 cm (standard frames); giclée at true proportions, long edge 30/45/60/90 cm | proportions locked; **never above the design's print ceiling** — its crop's pixels at ≥ 240 ppi (D26, C9 `printCeilingOf()`); editions never larger than the original |
| Paper (giclée) | smooth cotton rag ~308 gsm · textured etching ~310 gsm | close-up swatch; a paper sample kit product |
| Frame | none · slim black · natural teak · dark teak · antique gold | shows frame width and total outer size |
| Mount | none / 4 / 6 / 8 cm; warm white · ivory · black | only with a frame; never on canvas |
| Glazing | acrylic (default) · UV anti-reflective acrylic · glass | glass only for pickup or delivery within Bali |
| Personalise (v2) | caption ≤ 40 characters; map pin + a two-line title panel | brand font fixed; preview before ordering |
| Gift | gift note · hide prices on the slip (S10) — a wrapping cloth printed with an archive map only once the owner offers it | wrapping a priced line; the note and hidden prices free |

**The options are the page** — server-rendered radio groups in a GET form that
works without JavaScript, hydrated progressively; only the preview loads on intent
(DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §7). The destination's price table ships with the page, so the
price updates instantly on the client (display only — the bag re-prices on the
server).

**Everything it offers is in stock (S7, 2026-10-01).** At launch nothing is made to
order, so the configurator is a guide through **the variants the shop stocks**, not
a made-to-order builder. A product's variants are the combinations staff create in
the merch wizard (TASKS.md 24.1) and count into the shop's one stock pool, at the
showroom (COMMERCE.md §4) — and only those exist: every option the configurator shows belongs
to at least one stocked variant, and a combination nobody stocks is no variant and
is never offered. A stocked one that has sold out stays visible, saying so, with a
back-in-stock alert (`retention.backInStock`) — never a disabled button without its
reason. The framed options — frames, mounts, glazing — are therefore the framed
prints on the shelf, and the delivery promise reads stock (§4). The product type's
rules above still decide what the wizard may create: mount only with a frame, glass
only for pickup or delivery within Bali, and nothing above the design's print
ceiling (D26). Made to order — local production (COMMERCE.md §8) and "Print from the
Archive" (§6) — stays in the engine for when the owner offers it.

**Preview behaviour** — layered in the browser from a pre-sized ~1200 px AVIF,
9-slice frame layers, pre-composited room plates and an SVG scale view, updating
in under 100 ms without a reload on the reference device. Three views: **Flat**,
**On a wall** (three wall colours), **To scale** (a 1.7 m person and a 2 m sofa
with dimension lines). On a phone the preview stays pinned above a pull-up options
panel. The chosen options live in the URL, so a configuration can be sent on
WhatsApp or, by a signed-in partner, turned into a quote ("Turn this into a
quote" → the quote form, then the `Quote` surface; §9, D36 — a guest sees no
such action, and applies as a partner instead). Impossible combinations are disabled **with the reason shown**. The
preview is decoration over a text summary of the selection (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §9);
the price change is announced politely; swatches carry names and ≥ 24 px targets.

**Pricing** — one price table per product family shared by all artworks, times
the artwork multiplier, tax included — **in rupiah, and only rupiah** (S3: the shop
delivers within Indonesia only at launch, so no foreign estimate appears; COMMERCE.md
§3). When the owner opens export, D47's design applies: the shop's one seller is an
Indonesian PT (D2), which charges rupiah, so a buyer abroad would see each price as
an estimate in their market's currency beside the exact rupiah it is charged — "≈
€46 — charged in Rp 812.000" — and pay by card in that rupiah amount, or by PayPal,
which takes no rupiah, in the US dollars the payment step shows before the choice.
Separate market price lists (USD/EUR/AUD/SGD, built from production plus
duties-paid shipping, at clean price points) wait for a seller that can charge those
currencies — the v2 Singapore seller (§11). Bundles: print + frame, wall sets 10–15%
off, "3 for 2" cards.

## 6. Design pages — one image, many products

`/designs/{slug}`: the artwork large, its story and Archive No., the original's
status at the gallery, and **every product made from it** — print, poster,
postcard, tote, notebook, mug — as the Society6 / Rifle "shop by pattern" model

## 7. Bag and checkout

**Bag drawer:** line items with their configuration · a **free-shipping progress
bar** (S13: free over Rp 500.000, an automatic rule set in the admin) · a frame
upsell for unframed prints · postcard add-ons · the **voucher field** Indonesian
shoppers expect, the welcome code included · the delivery estimate for the
destination.

**Bag edge cases are designed, not discovered:** a line that becomes invalid
after a destination change (glass glazing is Bali-only) says why and offers
acrylic; a showroom item that sells out while in the bag; mixed fulfilment
(couriered stock + pickup) shows an estimate per shipment; every voucher failure
(expired, minimum spend, not combinable) is worded to instruct; the free-shipping
bar re-computes after a destination change; gift wrap, once offered, is per line or
per order, and says which; a visitor abroad is told, once, that the shop delivers
within Indonesia only for now (S3).

**Checkout — Indonesian destination:** WhatsApp number first (order updates
arrive there), normalised (`08xx` → `+62 8xx`) with a "this is my WhatsApp"
toggle · **one "Full name" field** (many Indonesians have one name) · address by
searchable pickers (province → city → district → sub-district), postcode filled
from the sub-district, **map pin optional** · couriers with price and ETA (JNE,
J&T, SiCepat; GoSend/GrabExpress same-day in Denpasar and Jakarta) · **showroom
pickup** · voucher · QRIS, e-wallets, virtual accounts that confirm automatically
(never "upload your transfer receipt"), cards, pay-later, Alfamart/Indomaret —
filtered by amount (PAYMENTS.md §3) · prices in **IDR only**.

**"I'm visiting Bali."** A tourist on a Bali IP gets two clear paths instead of
Indonesian defaults: deliver to my hotel or villa **before my departure date**
(S11), or collect at the showroom. Sending it home abroad is not offered at launch
(S3) — the page says so in one line, and packaging copy says what fits a suitcase
("packs flat, under 1 kg").

**Checkout — export destination: not at launch** (S3). When the owner opens
export: guest checkout · express wallets where the seller supports them · a
shipping estimate and the market's "≈" estimate from the ship-to selector · duties
included or clearly estimated · PayPal's dollar charge shown before the choice
(D47's design, COMMERCE.md §3).

**Payment pending** is a designed page (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §2): amount, VA number
with copy, per-bank steps, countdown, the bank-cap warning, "we'll WhatsApp you
when it's paid", automatic switch to paid; QRIS with "save QR to gallery" and
e-wallet deep links, because a phone cannot scan its own screen; failures retry
with another method and keep the bag. In an **in-app browser** (Instagram,
WhatsApp, TikTok) a payment step that cannot complete says so and offers "open in
your browser".

**Gifts:** a recipient address separate from the buyer's — in Indonesia at launch
(S3) — a preview of the gift note, a packing slip with prices hidden (S10: a gift
note only, no wrapping until the owner offers it), a target delivery date (before
Lebaran). No gift wrap and no gift card at launch (S10: a gift note only) — the
digital gift card, sent to a recipient by email on a chosen date with a
balance-check page, stays in the engine (`commerce.giftCards`) for when the owner
offers one.

**After the order:** guests track by order number plus email or WhatsApp number
(`OrderLookup`), with the courier timeline and the tracking link in every
WhatsApp update; pickups get a code or QR, a "ready" notice, the showroom's hours,
a map and who may collect. A print that arrives damaged is replaced on a photo
claim (S12) — the owner's promise; no refund and no change-of-mind return is the
owner's intention, published only in counsel's words (COMPLIANCE.md §6).

## 8. The showroom, Instagram and WhatsApp

- **The showroom is the shop's one stock location at launch** — every unit the
  site sells is counted in one pool there (COMMERCE.md §4) — and every part of it is
  on at launch (S4, 2026-10-01): **published opening hours** (the owner gives them),
  **QRIS at the counter** — staff ring up a walk-in's purchase as a showroom sale
  against the pool in the admin (TASKS.md 24.4.c) and the buyer pays the QRIS it
  shows, while a visitor who scans a label and checks out on their own phone pays
  QRIS there — **pickup of online orders** from the pool, and **QR labels** on the
  walls that open each product page in the showroom mode (§4). No "In the showroom
  now" badge or filter at launch: with one pool it would say nothing "in stock" does
  not. The merchandise also sells through hundreds of partner shops across Bali,
  which report sales and restocking over WhatsApp as today; selling from their
  shelves on this engine — their stock, a till, how each holds it — is the
  point-of-sale phase (backlog). (v2) buy in the showroom and ship home, printed
  near the buyer with duties paid. Walk-ins are the main buyers today (S9). Its own
  photographs — the street and the entrance, a wide view, each wall, the counter,
  a few vignettes, the making (`docs/design/imagery/shop-guide.md` §5) — are the
  location's images (CONTENT-MODEL.md §2), photographs only, never a mockup; the
  visit band and a pickup's instructions lead with the entrance, so a visitor
  recognises it on arrival.
- **Instagram:** an `/ig` page on the shop's own domain replaces the Linktree —
  recent posts, each linked to its products. One Meta catalogue feed drives
  Instagram product tags **and** the WhatsApp catalogue, so the Drive PDFs can
  go. Checkout always happens on the site (Permendag 31/2023: social media may
  promote, not take payment).
- **WhatsApp** is a first-class channel, with **its own number for online
  shoppers** (S6), separate from the showroom's: "Ask on WhatsApp" on every product
  and in the shell uses the online number (C1 `identity.contact.whatsapp`), with
  context prefilled **in the page's language** (from the brand lexicon) and its
  reply hours stated beside it (the owner gives the number and the hours; until
  then no hours are promised); the showroom's own number is the location's
  (CONTENT-MODEL.md §2) — on the Visit page and a pickup's instructions. After-hours
  auto-reply, order and shipping updates, and B2B quotes. A payment link a staff member sends on
  WhatsApp opens a designed `Pay` page — item, terms, expiry, the seller's
  identity — never a bare gateway screen.

## 9. Partnership

**One programme for every business buyer** (D31, D36). Retail shops, hotels,
villas, cafés, embassies and companies all apply as partners; there is no
separate For Business path. The shop already supplies 100+ shops, so the
**Partnership** page — reached from a highlight in the home hero and from the
header — says what a partner gets, for each kind of business — quotes built for
it, trade pricing agreed with the shop, delivery and framing — and ends in the one
application and partner sign-in, which work without JavaScript. **It publishes no
discount and no minimum order** (D32 answered, S5: terms are case by case): staff
agree each partner's terms with it, and the admin records them — a price tier and a
minimum order as data, set per partner, changed only by the owner role (D33, D37).
Partners are the **only** accounts on this site: an application approved by staff,
then a partner area showing that partner's own terms, where orders are quotes,
built by staff in the order builder, and every past order reorders in one click
(TASKS.md 28.5). An ended partnership deactivates the account: its orders stay with
the owner (D34).

A partner's order is a brief in its own words — quantities, sizes, framing,
deadlines — that becomes a **quote page** (lines at its tier beside the list
prices, validity, PDF, accept → payment link — the `Quote` surface); a partner
can also turn any configured product into a quote ("Turn this into a quote").
Corporate gift sets, custom sizes and murals are quoted the same way. v2: a trade
portal with self-serve ordering and invoices. The research's standout
opportunity: the Bali Hotel in the posters still operates as Inna Bali in central
Denpasar — a co-branded collection, a lobby corner, room art with QR story
cards.
