### Observations (product, not test)

1. **Deleted media leave public objects.** Deleting a media record does not remove its derivatives or tile pyramid
   from the public bucket, so they stay reachable at their URLs. Suggested owner: media/CMS tier (an `afterDelete`
   that removes `derivatives/v1/<asset>/` and `iiif/<asset>/`).
2. **A deleted work's page outlives it in the cache.** After a work was deleted, the item address (its `publicId`,
   reused by the next work) kept serving the deleted work's page for about 40 s (stale-while-revalidate). The same
   path is exercised by 5.3sold (sold/unpublish freshness).
3. **Keyboard zoom is feeble.** One `+` tap is about 1.1× (OpenSeadragon 6 per-frame zoom); the hint "Keys: +, −"
   suggests a step. UX tier, low.
4. At 390 px the header shows the "Indies Gallery" logo lockup **and** the wordmark text beside it, wrapping onto
   two lines (visible in every screenshot). UI tier, low.
5. The fixture image is a hand-built PNG with no EXIF, because `sharp` cannot be resolved from `tests/e2e/`. The
   ticket's GPS-EXIF clause is not exercised here; F3 is covered by `media.pipeline.storage.db.test.ts`.
